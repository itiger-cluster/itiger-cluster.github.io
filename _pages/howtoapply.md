iTiger cluster requires users to create accounts in order to gain access. This process entails filling out a Microsoft Form in the [Registration Requests page]({% link _pages/requests.md %}). University of Memphis (UofM) provides cybersecurity and privacy protection (e.g., firewall and multi-factor authentication) and requires a UofM account to access its Internet services. If you are not on the university network, or you are an external user accessing the iTiger cluster, you need to install and use the [UofM VPN](https://www.memphis.edu/umtech/solutions/vpn.php). Please read the following guide. **Disclaimer**: we will try our best to reply to your requests in a timely manner, but due to the volume of requests and limited staff support, please be patient.

## What is an Internal or External Applicant? 

**An internal applicant** is an applicant who has an affiliation with the University of Memphis. If you are an internal applicant, you are either a current student, faculty member or staff member of the University of Memphis. 

**An external applicant** is an applicant who does NOT have an affiliation with the University of Memphis. To access the cluster, you need to obtain a UofM account. See the instructions below.

## How do I Gain An Account? 

If you are an internal applicant or have a UofM account, fill out the form in our [Registration Requests page]({% link _pages/requests.md %}). Select the "Internal applicant" choice.

If you are an external applicant, read below: 

As an external applicant, you will need to have a UofM faculty member offer you a sponsored account. When you connect or collaborate with the UofM faculty, have them view the **[IT instruction](https://www.memphis.edu/its/accounts/sponsored.php)** or go to the [UofM service desk](https://umhelpdesk.memphis.edu/) and type "sponsored account" into the search bar to submit a ticket request. From there, your faculty collaborator can request a sponsored account for you. 

In addition, fill out the form in our [Registration Requests page]({% link _pages/requests.md %}), but select yourself as an "External" applicant and enter your e-mail.

## Next Steps? 
After you receive your account, your next steps are to install the required software (potentially a VPN as well). You may follow the ["Creating an Account" tutorial]({% post_url 2024-03-26-account %}) to continue getting started with the HPC.
