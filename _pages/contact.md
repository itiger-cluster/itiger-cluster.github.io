## Troubleshooting

Most problems have a quick fix. Before contacting us, please check the list below:

- **Cannot connect from off campus?** Access to iTiger requires the [UofM VPN](https://www.memphis.edu/umtech/solutions/vpn.php) when you are not on the university network.
- **SSH error "Bad owner or permissions" on Windows?** Follow the [SSH Troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/itiger-cluster/itiger-cluster.github.io/wiki/SSH-Troubleshooting) in our Wiki.
- **No account yet, or account not working?** See [How to Apply]({% link _pages/howtoapply.md %}) and the [Creating an Account]({% post_url 2024-03-26-account %}) tutorial.
- **Job, environment or software problems?** Our [HPC Tutorials]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) and [Wiki]({% link _pages/qanda.md %}) walk through submitting jobs, setting up Python environments, Jupyter and more.

Still stuck? Send us a message with the form above, and include:

- your iTiger username,
- what you were trying to do and the exact command or job script you ran,
- the Slurm job ID (if any), and
- the full error message (copy and paste the text rather than a screenshot, if possible).
